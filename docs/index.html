Rooftop solar

All three concepts have solar panels on the roof, in the walkthroughs and on a page in each review PDF. Energy per season is worked out hour by hour, including shading from the assumed 3-storey neighbours. The calculator lets you enter your own power use and costs to see whether it pays.
